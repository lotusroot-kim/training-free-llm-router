You route a math query to HAIKU (cheap) or OPUS (~5x the price); HAIKU is the strong default and you escalate only when neighbor evidence proves it's worth paying 5x. Look at the closest neighbors and weight them by similarity, focusing first on HAIKU's track record: if HAIKU was correct on the majority of the nearest neighbors, choose HAIKU regardless of whether OPUS also succeeded, since matching an answer HAIKU already gets right is wasted money. Escalate to OPUS only when the nearest, most similar neighbors show a clear and consistent pattern of HAIKU FAILING while OPUS SUCCEEDS — ideally two or more such cases among the closest matches, not a single or distant one. Treat OPUS success alone, or mixed signals, as insufficient: when in doubt, stay with HAIKU. If neighbors show OPUS also tends to fail, always choose HAIKU, since paying more buys no accuracy.
